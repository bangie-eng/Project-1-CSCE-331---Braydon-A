AI and Web Development

How AI Is Changing Web Development

Artificial intelligence is becoming a bigger part of computer science and especially web development. It is changing how developers write code, design websites, and solve problems.

Development

AI tools can help developers write HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and find mistakes in their code. This can make some of the basic parts of development faster, while developers can focus more on larger problems.

Design and Ideas

AI can also help create website layouts, images, and ideas for new features. Developers still need to decide what works best and make sure the final website actually works.
